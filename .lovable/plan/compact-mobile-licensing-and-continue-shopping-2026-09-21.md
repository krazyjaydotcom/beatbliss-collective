# Compact mobile licensing and continue shopping

## Changes
- Remove the always-visible selected-license bullet list above the cart and purchase actions.
- Add a **License details** link within every license option; opening it reveals only that license’s details without changing the selected license.
- Keep the license cards compact so **Add to cart** and **Buy now** sit higher on mobile.
- Add a **Continue shopping** action to the cart that closes it and returns shoppers to the beat list without clearing their selections.
