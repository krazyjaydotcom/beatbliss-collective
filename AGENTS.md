- Bulk/marketing email goes through SendFox via admin-only server functions in src/lib/sendfox.functions.ts (token from SENDFOX_API_TOKEN, drafts only, web_publish=false); personal one-off emails never go through SendFox — keeps marketing separate from transactional mail.
- Personal one-off emails are only sent as replies to a specific inquiry (src/lib/inquiry-reply.functions.ts, 'inquiry-reply' app-email template): recipient loaded server-side from the inquiry row, admin-only, every attempt logged in inquiry_replies — the app-email service forbids promotional or list sends.
- One-person emails with real attachments go through Amazon SES v2 via admin-only src/lib/ses.functions.ts (aws4fetch SigV4, creds only in AWS_SES_* server secrets, private email-attachments bucket, every attempt in ses_send_log); SES is personal/customer-matter only, never promotional or bulk (no automated unsubscribe link) — promotional and bulk stay SendFox, inquiry replies stay on the app-email template.
- Paid beat licenses are stored in purchase_licenses by the payment webhook (guest buyers have no user_id); the public /licenses page only emails copies to the entered address, never displays them — prevents lookup of other people's purchases.
